
des_des.zip:

This archive contains five games by Design Design Software: Tank Busters,
Halls of the Things '85, Dark Star, Forbidden Planet and Bat & Ball.

These games were donated for unmodified free-of-charge distribution 
by their author, Simon Brattel (crem@cix.compulink.co.uk).

They were saved as 64 K snapshots under CPCEMU 1.2 emulating a CPC6128.
